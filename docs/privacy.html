Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Overview

Packet is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in the Packet application.

Data Collection

Packet operates on a zero-knowledge principle:

  • No Logs: We do not collect, store, or log user activity, connection data, or traffic.
  • No Metadata: We do not track when you connect or disconnect.
  • No Telemetry: We do not send usage analytics or diagnostic data.
  • Client-Side Keys: Encryption keys are generated and stored on your device only.

Encryption

All data transmitted through Packet is encrypted end-to-end using XChaCha20-Poly1305. The server cannot decrypt or inspect your traffic.

Third-Party Services

Packet is a standalone application and does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Open Source

The client applications for Android and iOS are open source. You can review the complete source code on GitHub.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page.
