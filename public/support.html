Packet Support

Last Updated: May 18, 2026

Contact

Email support at support@packetapp.net.

What to Include

  • Your app version and platform.
  • The connection status shown in Packet.
  • Whether the server endpoint is your own server, a CDN edge, or DirectSock.
  • Any error text from the Packet diagnostics screen. Do not send your shared secret.

Privacy

Packet does not ask for your shared secret during support. Keep credentials and private server keys on your own device.
